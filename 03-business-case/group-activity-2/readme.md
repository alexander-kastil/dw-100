# Group Activity 2: Business Case Builder Practice

## Get ready for Group Activity 2

Download [Business Case Builder Excel Worksheet](https://aka.ms/BusinessCaseBuilder) if you have access to the Partner Center, otherwise get it from [resources](/resources/05%20-%20Group%20Activity%202%20-%20Business%20Case%20Builder.xlsm).

## Using the Business Case Builder

- Take the output from the previous group activity and use the Business Case Builder to create a business case for your customer.

- If possible share your screen and walk through the process with your group. Give each group member a chance to contribute to the business case or practice building one on their own.

- Use the [Guide to the Business Case Builder](/resources/05%20-%20Group%20Activity%20Guidance.pdf) to help you through the process.
